How to tell them apart

  • parser: the day and month swap. A day-first tenant’s date goes through the shared parser and comes out month first: 7 April becomes 4 July. It reaches us through the app, email and forms that print the tenant’s own order.
  • hint: also a swap, but the parser is not to blame. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type what it tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not touch it; fixing the hint does.
  • move: not a swap. The date is one day off (“Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May”), or two people in different cities see different days for one meeting. The desk already read these apart (desk notes); they were filed under parser only because of their tenant. Day and month order cannot move a date by one day, so this is a third bug, most likely in how the time zone is applied.
